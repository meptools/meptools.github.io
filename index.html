    About MEPtools

    Calculators and tools for everyday MEP work. Works offline once installed. Nothing you type leaves your device.

    • Equal-friction / velocity sizing: enter any two of flow rate, head loss, velocity and equivalent diameter.
    • Method: Darcy-Weisbach with Colebrook-White friction factor; target head loss is solved with the Swamee-Jain explicit form, then the result is checked with Colebrook-White. Roughness 0.0007 ft (0.213 mm).
    • Rectangular ducts: converted to the equivalent round duct for the same friction and airflow (Huebscher, as in ASHRAE).
    • HVAC calculators: Air Change / Hour, diffuser and valve quantities, linear diffuser, grill size, louvers, sand trap, AC capacity converter, Y branch and area ratio. Reference tables can be edited and restored.
    • Converters: 22 unit converters (flow, pressure, temperature, power, cooling capacity, U-value and more) plus Pipe Size, Sheet Gauge and Electrical Load. The last selections are remembered.
    • Pipe Sizer: pick the material, size, fluid and flow to get the pipe properties and the friction head loss (Darcy-Weisbach with Colebrook / Swamee-Jain). Pipe data: ASME B36.10 (steel), ASTM B88 (copper), ASTM D1785 (PVC/ABS).
    • Chilled Water Calci: sizes Schedule 40 steel pipe by design head loss (quick select 3, 4 or 5 ft per 100 ft, or any typed value) or by velocity, using the equal-friction method (ASHRAE practice). Velocity and head loss can be edited.
    • Manhole Schedule: enter the IL of the first manhole, then the distance from the connected manhole for each next one. IL = previous IL − distance ÷ N − backdrop (Up to Down). Depth = CL − IL; manhole and cover sizes come from the standard table and can be changed. Print (A3 landscape) and Excel export.
    • Rain Water Calci: Q uses the correct Metric or IP formula; RWO is based on 272 m² (approximately 2,927.78 ft²) per outlet as a planning reference.
    • Air: standard air and four humid-air conditions (Metric and IP each have their own list).
    • Head loss is on the standard-air chart basis; velocity pressure and Reynolds number use the selected air.

    Results were checked against reference duct-sizer values (within about 0.05%). Always confirm against your project standards before issuing drawings.
